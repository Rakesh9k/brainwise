## 1. Executive Summary

BrainWise Analytics & Reports is intended to serve as the central performance and decision intelligence layer for businesses across various business models (SaaS, Ecommerce, D2C/FMCG, Restaurants, Salons, Agencies, Professional Services, and Startups).

The existing implementation was thoroughly inspected across frontend components (`src/pages/app/AnalyticsReportsPage.tsx`, `src/components/ui/GrowthTrendChart.tsx`, `src/utils/logic.ts`), backend routes (`src/server/routes/v1/executiveReportRoutes.ts`), services (`unifiedReportService.ts`, `performanceIngestionService.ts`, `growthAnalyticsEngine.ts`), repositories (`businessMetricRepository.ts`, `campaignPerformanceRepository.ts`, `executiveReportRepository.ts`), database collections, and integration providers (`googleAnalytics4Provider.ts`).

### Key Audit Finding
While the foundational scaffolding for event-based metrics and high-level 7-section dossiers exists, **the current Analytics & Reports module is predominantly simulated on the frontend and lacks a deterministic metric foundation on the backend**:
1. **Frontend Mocking:** `AnalyticsReportsPage.tsx` relies on hardcoded data series (`MONTHLY_DEMO_SERIES`, `ANALYTICS_MRR_SERIES`) and mock attribution cards. It is disconnected from the backend metric repositories and executive report APIs.
2. **Missing Canonical Metric Registry:** Different services calculate metrics with ad-hoc formulas, silently replace missing data with zeros, and use crude heuristic rules (such as `sampleSize >= 30` as a universal statistical test).
3. **Absence of Reconciliation & Data Health:** External feeds are not validated against source manifests; there is no reconciliation engine for transactions, refunds, or duplicates.
4. **No Guardrailed AI Synthesis for Reports:** Reports generated by `executiveReportRoutes.ts` use static text templates and do not validate numeric assertions against verified metric snapshots.
5. **No Decision Closed-Loop:** Strategic recommendations from reports or growth engines are not linked to measurable baselines, targets, review dates, or post-implementation outcome assessments.

## 3. Data-Flow and Integration Inventory

### 3.1 Existing Data Flows
1. **Workspace Activity Flow:**
   `Tasks/Goals/Experiments` in Firestore -> `executiveReportRoutes.ts` -> reads array counts -> creates summary string in `ExecutiveReportEntity`.
2. **Metric Ingestion Flow:**
   Raw metric payload -> `performanceIngestionService.ts` -> SHA-256 hash idempotency check -> saved in `businessMetrics` collection -> NO reconciliation with source manifests.
3. **Unified Dossier Flow:**
   `POST /reports/dossier/generate` -> queries 6 department services in parallel -> generates deterministic financial models, brand ladders, and competitive positioning -> saves to Firestore -> exports print HTML/CSV.

## 4. Deep Defects & Gaps Identification

### 4.1 Defect 1: Client-Side Simulation & Design Freeze Violation
- `AnalyticsReportsPage.tsx` explicitly renders `"Simulated Feed"` and uses hardcoded series `ANALYTICS_MRR_SERIES` and `MONTHLY_DEMO_SERIES`.
- The user is shown demo numbers ($1,680 MRR, 36.0% Trial->Sub, 12,500 reach) even when real workspace data exists.
- Export PDF in frontend is merely `window.print()` instead of an executive PDF/HTML export engine.

### 4.2 Defect 2: Universal Metric Registry Missing
- No central metric registry defining canonical formulas, numerators, denominators, business-model relevance, and units.
- In `growthAnalyticsEngine.ts`, missing fields are silently defaulted to `0` (`monthlyRevenue: currentMetrics.monthlyRevenue || 0`). This violates the core principle: **"Never silently convert missing values to zero."**
- Metric states (`VERIFIED`, `PARTIALLY_VERIFIED`, `ESTIMATED`, `MODELED`, `FORECAST`, `INSUFFICIENT_DATA`, `STALE`, `UNAVAILABLE`, `INVALID`) do not exist.

### 4.3 Defect 3: Naive Statistical Evaluation (`N >= 30` Fallacy)
- `growthAnalyticsEngine.ts` (line 74) uses `const isStatisticallySignificant = sampleSize >= 30`.
- In reality, sample size adequacy depends on base conversion rate, Minimum Detectable Effect (MDE), statistical power ($1-\beta$), and significance level ($\alpha$). Using $N \ge 30$ universally leads to false positives and hazardous business decisions.

### 4.4 Defect 4: Financial & Revenue Integrity Gaps
- No distinction between Recognized Revenue, Booked Revenue, Cash Collected, and Gross/Net Revenue.
- No automated handling of refunds, chargebacks, discounts, tax withholdings, or multi-currency conversions.
- Missing business-model-specific packages (e.g., MRR waterfall for SaaS vs AOV/Repeat Purchase for Ecommerce vs Utilization/Peak-Period for Restaurants and Salons).

### 4.5 Defect 5: Marketing Attribution Gaps
- Only basic CPA and ROAS are computed.
- No multi-touch attribution models (First-touch, Last-touch, Linear, Time-decay, Position-based/U-shaped).
- No accounting for unattributed revenue or cookie consent limitations.

### 4.6 Defect 6: AI Synthesis & Report Reproducibility Gaps
- Executive reports in `executiveReportRoutes.ts` do not validate numeric claims against immutable metric snapshots.
- No cryptographic hash or calculation version is stored on report entities to ensure historical immutability.

### 4.7 Defect 7: Lack of Closed-Loop Decision Intelligence
- Strategic recommendations do not track baseline metrics, targets, review dates, or post-implementation variance.

## 5. Security & Multi-Tenancy Audit

1. **Workspace Boundary:** All Firestore queries in `executiveReportRepository`, `businessMetricRepository`, and `unifiedReportService` enforce `workspaces/{workspaceId}/...`.
2. **ADR-006 Credit Integrity:** `executiveReportRoutes.ts` implements two-phase reservation (`FEAT-REP-001`, 5 credits) and settlement. If generation fails, `refundCredits` is called.
3. **Gap:** No rate limiting or payload validation specific to high-volume metric ingestion streams.
4. **Gap:** No audit logging when financial snapshots are recalculated or reconciled.
