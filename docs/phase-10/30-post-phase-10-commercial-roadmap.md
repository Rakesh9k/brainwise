## 3. Detailed Stage Objectives & Success Gates

### Stage 11: Controlled Beta Launch (Invite-Only Pilot)
- **Primary Objective:** Validate that real, unassisted businesses can onboard, configure their Business Brain, execute marketing and intelligence tasks, and purchase credit packs without engineering or customer support intervention.
- **Cohort Size:** 25 curated SME businesses (5 B2B SaaS, 10 D2C Retailers, 5 Marketing Agencies, 5 Professional Services).
- **Core Verification Gates:**
  1. **Zero-Touch Onboarding:** $\ge 80\%$ of pilot users complete the 6-step Business Brain onboarding without dropping off or contacting support.
  2. **First-Session Value Delivery:** Every onboarded business receives at least one actionable marketing plan, competitive audit, or high-DPI vector collateral piece within 10 minutes of registration.
  3. **Live Gateway Transactions:** Successful end-to-end processing of real credit pack purchases via Cashfree in Indian Rupees (INR) with automated GST tax invoices issued.
  4. **Support Ticket Threshold:** $< 0.1$ tickets per active user session.

---

### Stage 12: Customer Acquisition & Autonomous Onboarding
- **Primary Objective:** Transition from invite-only beta to self-serve public commercial availability with automated marketing funnels and self-healing onboarding.
- **Target Scale:** First 500 paying business workspaces.
- **Core Focus Areas:**
  - **Self-Serve Conversion Funnel:** Optimize public landing page conversion (`https://brainwise.ai`) with interactive product tours and sample intelligence dossiers.
  - **Payment Funnel Reliability:** 99.8% checkout success rate on Cashfree, supporting UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), credit/debit cards, and net banking.
  - **Automated Credit Lifecycle:** Autonomous notifications for low credit balances (at 20% and 5% remaining), one-click credit pack reorders, and seamless plan upgrades.
  - **Onboarding Drop-off Diagnostics:** Telemetry identifying confusion points in the onboarding flow, triggering contextual guidance modals.

---

### Stage 13: Usage Analytics & Product Optimization
- **Primary Objective:** Mine production operational telemetry to understand which intelligence features drive high retention, optimize model inference costs, and minimize credit churn.
- **Key Metrics Tracked:**
  - **Credit Consumption Velocity:** Daily and weekly credit burn rate by department (Brand Studio vs. Competitive Intelligence vs. Growth Director).
  - **Angle Fatigue Index:** Measuring how often users re-generate campaign angles vs. accepting first-pass outputs.
  - **Truth Check Rejection Rate:** Tracking if users attempt to introduce unsupported pricing claims or unconfirmed facts, refining prompt guardrails.
  - **Retention & Churn:** 30-day and 90-day workspace retention rates; identifying signals that precede subscription cancellations.

---

### Stage 14: Scaling & Advanced Autonomous Capabilities
- **Primary Objective:** Scale infrastructure to support thousands of concurrent tenants, expand multi-model routing intelligence, and deepen automation capabilities.
- **Key Enhancements:**
  - **Multi-Region Cloud Run Deployments:** Low-latency API gateways across Mumbai (`asia-south1`), Delhi (`asia-south2`), and Singapore (`asia-southeast1`).
  - **Dynamic Model Cascading:** Real-time routing between Gemini 1.5 Pro and Gemini 1.5 Flash based on prompt complexity, reducing token latency by $40\%$.
  - **Autonomous External Sync:** Broaden background sync connectors (Shopify, Meta Ads, GA4) with automated anomaly detection alerts delivered to user dashboards.
  - **Continued Strict Video Exclusion:** Affirming the permanent commitment that BrainWise remains strictly focused on high-DPI vector collateral, print PDFs, carousels, and strategic text intelligence.
