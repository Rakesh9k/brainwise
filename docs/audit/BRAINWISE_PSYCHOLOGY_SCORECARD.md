## 1. Scoring Methodology & Rubric

In accordance with master audit instructions, every psychological principle is rigorously scored across five distinct dimensions rather than relying on subjective intuition:

$$\text{Principle Score} = (I \times 0.25) + (UX \times 0.25) + (PE \times 0.20) + (TR \times 0.15) + (M \times 0.15)$$

- **Implementation Quality ($I$, 25%):** Completeness, architectural rigor, and depth of source code implementation.
- **User Experience Quality ($UX$, 25%):** Visual hierarchy, responsiveness, clarity, and ergonomics of the interface.
- **Psychological Effectiveness ($PE$, 20%):** Does the feature achieve its intended cognitive or behavioral outcome?
- **Technical Reliability ($TR$, 15%):** Robustness, error handling, performance latency, and test validation.
- **Measurability & Optimization ($M$, 15%):** Event telemetry, observability, and ability to A/B test or optimize.

### Scoring Scale:
- **0–1:** Completely missing or broken
- **2–3:** Very weak implementation
- **4–5:** Partially implemented
- **6–7:** Good implementation with gaps
- **8–9:** Excellent implementation
- **10:** Exceptional, validated by real-world user behavioral evidence

## 5. Master Summary & Strategic Takeaway

With a master 15-principle score of **7.95 / 10** and an additional areas score of **7.93 / 10**, BrainWise stands as a remarkably sophisticated, ethically grounded business intelligence platform.

### Strongest Psychological Pillars:
1. **Trust and Psychological Safety (9.13 / 10):** The Two-Phase Credit Protocol, deterministic Truth Check engine, and transparent Fact Provenance tagging represent an industry benchmark for enterprise AI credibility.
2. **Personalization Effect (8.65 / 10):** Generative outputs dynamically reference the user's real business facts, eliminating generic AI cliches.
3. **Emotional Ownership (8.58 / 10):** The persistent Business Brain creates deep founder attachment through accumulated corporate memory.

### Weakest Psychological Pillars:
1. **Habit Loop External Cues (6.95 / 10):** While the internal routine is frictionless, the absence of automated external triggers (email digests, Slack webhooks) limits recurring engagement when the browser is closed.
2. **Variable Rewards Automation (7.13 / 10):** The platform requires the user to manually trigger scans rather than proactively delivering surprise intelligence insights.
3. **Social Proof & Advocacy (7.0 / 10):** Lacks viral referral loops and live customer evidence badges.
