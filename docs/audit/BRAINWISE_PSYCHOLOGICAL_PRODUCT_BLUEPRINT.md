## 3. The Ideal Personalized Experience (The Living Brain)

BrainWise must never forget. It adapts to the user’s reality across three interconnected dimensions:

1. **Persistent Memory of Truth:** If the founder changes their pricing from ₹999 to ₹1,499 in Business Brain, that price immediately updates across all 14 departments. Brand Studio never generates an ad with the old price; Growth Director never plans an experiment based on obsolete margins.
2. **Anti-Fatigue Generative Diversity:** BrainWise tracks every angle, hook, and aesthetic style previously used (`strategicMemory.ts`). When the user asks for new marketing collateral, BrainWise automatically excludes tired angles, forcing fresh, high-converting contrast.
3. **Adaptive Tone of Voice:** A luxury wellness retreat receives soothing, editorial, high-status language; a cybersecurity SaaS receives direct, risk-focused, forensic technical copy.

## 9. The 10 Permanent Product Design Principles

Every future feature, screen, and API endpoint added to BrainWise must strictly comply with these 10 inviolable laws:

1. **The Ground-Truth Mandate:** No AI feature may generate copy, pricing, or strategic claims without referencing the user’s Business Brain.
2. **Absolute Video Ban:** Never introduce AI video generation, video editing, video rendering, or video credit tiers. Focus purely on high-craft vector SVG, typography, static multi-frame carousels, and print collateral.
3. **Explicit Provenance Transparency:** Always distinguish `USER_CONFIRMED` facts from `AI_INFERRED` hypotheses. Never present an AI assumption as an absolute fact.
4. **Two-Phase Financial Integrity:** Never decrement credits on the client. Always hold credits prior to AI jobs and immediately release them upon failure or Truth Check rejection.
5. **Strict 1.25 Modular Typographic Scale:** Maintain the mathematical 1.25 modular scale anchored at 16px. Never introduce arbitrary font sizes.
6. **No Blank Canvases:** Never present a user with an empty screen. Always provide pre-populated intelligence, industry seeds, or progressive disclosure.
7. **Action Over Advice:** Never deliver raw analysis without an immediate next action. Every market report must culminate in an accepted sprint task or experiment.
8. **Anti-Fatigue Generative Variety:** Never repeat exhausted marketing angles. Always enforce cross-campaign diversity via strategic memory.
9. **Zero Dark Patterns:** Never use fake countdown timers, manufactured scarcity, hidden renewal terms, or hostages in cancellation flows.
10. **The Executive Glance Rule:** Every department must convey its core status and primary directive within 3 seconds via the Universal Executive Layer.
