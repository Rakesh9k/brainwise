## 1. Commercial Philosophy & Market Positioning

BrainWise is an intelligent business operating platform engineered for founders, operators, growth directors, and agency strategists. 

Traditional fragmented SaaS stacks force businesses to pay $50–$200/mo for SEO trackers, $30–$80/mo for AI copywriting, $20–$50/mo for competitor tracking, and $50–$100/mo for brand asset tools—totaling over ₹25,000 to ₹40,000 monthly across 5 disconnected tabs.

BrainWise unifies all 7 core departments (Business Brain, Idea Intelligence, Competitive Intelligence, Market Watch, Brand Studio, Growth Planner, Analytics & Reports) on a shared intelligence memory.

### Key Pricing Mandates:
1. **Entry Point:** Starts precisely from **₹2,763 / month** for early-stage builders and solo founders.
2. **Hero Offering ("The No-Brainer"):** **Professional Plan** is positioned with the highest perceived value-to-cost ratio, delivering 3.75x the credits, full integration access, and automated scheduled reporting for just 2.5x the price of Starter.
3. **Strict Video Exclusion:** Zero video generation, video editing, or video credits in the matrix.
4. **Predictable Financial Margin:** Guaranteed 85% to 95% gross margin across all AI inference and third-party API operations.

## 6. Financial Safety Controls

1. **No Negative Balances:** The credit ledger enforces `wallet.balance - wallet.reserved >= requiredCredits`. Under no circumstances can a user overdraw their balance.
2. **Two-Phase Reservation Lock:** All operations lock credits *before* invoking Gemini. If upstream inference fails or the Deterministic Truth Check detects an ungrounded claim, credits are refunded atomically.
3. **Separate Purchased vs Subscription Pools:** Purchased top-up credits never expire with monthly subscription resets; they roll over automatically.
4. **GST Tax Compliance:** The pricing engine separates base price from applicable 18% GST, generating compliant tax invoices with customer GSTIN recording.
