## 1. Commercial Plan Tiers

### 1.1 Free Starter Tier (₹0 / $0)
- **Target Audience:** Curious founders exploring the Business Brain and evaluating business validation.
- **Price:** ₹0 (Perpetual).
- **Welcome Allowance:** 150 one-time welcome credits.
- **Limits:** 1 workspace, 1 team seat, 3 idea dossiers / mo, 2 tracked competitors, 10 brand assets, 1 external integration (Google Research), standard AI models.

### 1.2 Starter Plan (₹2,763 / month)
- **Target Audience:** Solo founders and early-stage entrepreneurs validating and launching a single product line.
- **Pricing:**
  - Monthly: ₹2,763 / month ($35 / month).
  - Annual: ₹2,210 / month billed annually at ₹26,520 ($28 / month billed annually at $336) — **Save 20%**.
- **Credit Allowance:** 800 credits refreshed monthly.
- **Limits:** 1 workspace, 2 team seats, 10 idea dossiers / mo, 5 tracked competitors with daily diff alerts, 50 brand assets, 3 active platform integrations, 2 active automations, weekly scheduled executive briefing.

### 1.3 Professional Plan (₹6,999 / month) — HERO / MOST POPULAR
- **Target Audience:** Scaling brands, high-growth startups, and specialized creative studios.
- **Pricing:**
  - Monthly: ₹6,999 / month ($89 / month).
  - Annual: ₹5,599 / month billed annually at ₹67,188 ($71 / month billed annually at $852) — **Save 20%**.
- **Credit Allowance:** 3,000 credits refreshed monthly.
- **Limits:** 3 Business Brain workspaces, 5 team seats, unlimited idea dossiers, 20 tracked competitors with automated teardowns, 250 brand assets, all 9 external integrations, 10 active automations, priority Gemini 2.5 Flash / Pro reasoning models, white-label PDF executive reports.

### 1.4 Business Enterprise Plan (₹16,499 / month)
- **Target Audience:** Multi-brand operators, holdings, agencies, and high-velocity corporate product teams.
- **Pricing:**
  - Monthly: ₹16,499 / month ($209 / month).
